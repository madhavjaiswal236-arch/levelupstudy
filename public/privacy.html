Welcome to LevelUp Study (Tracker 360). Your privacy and the security of your data are paramount to us. This Privacy Policy outlines what information we collect, how it is used, how it is stored, and your rights concerning your personal data when using our mobile and web applications.

1. Information We Collect

A. Account Information (Google Authentication)

When you choose to sign in with Google, we collect minimal profile details provided by Google OAuth via Firebase Authentication:

  • Name and Email Address: Used to create and uniquely identify your student profile, allow cross-device cloud synchronization, and communicate critical service updates.
  • Profile Picture URL: Used solely to display your avatar within the application dashboard.
  • Unique User ID (UID): Generated by Firebase to associate your study records with your account securely.

B. Academic & Study Progress Data

To deliver JEE tracking, syllabus analytics, and backlog planning, our application collects the study data you input directly:

  • Tasks, study plans, syllabus checklist completion states, and backlog schedules.
  • Daily question solving statistics (e.g. attempted questions, accuracy rates, and session durations).
  • Gamification statistics including experience points (XP), preparation levels, and study streaks.

C. Google Calendar and Tasks Integration (Optional)

If you explicitly grant optional permissions, our application requests access to:

  • Google Calendar (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar): To create and sync your scheduled revision blocks and exam milestones directly into your personal calendar.
  • Google Tasks (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/tasks): To synchronize your daily study tasks and backlog todos with your Google Tasks account.

Google API Services User Data Policy Compliance:
LevelUp Study's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information exclusively to provide, maintain, and enhance the LevelUp Study experience:

  • To authenticate your identity across your web browser and Android mobile devices.
  • To save and synchronize your syllabus progress, study sessions, and backlog recovery plans in Cloud Firestore.
  • To compute deterministic study coach recommendations and exam readiness metrics.
  • To synchronize study events with Google Calendar and Google Tasks when requested by you.

3. How We Share and Disclose Data

We never sell, rent, monetize, or trade your personal or academic information to third parties, advertisers, or data brokers.

Your data is only processed by trusted infrastructure service providers required to operate the app:

  • Google Cloud Platform & Google Firebase: Provides secure cloud database storage (Cloud Firestore) and user authentication (Firebase Auth).

4. Data Storage and Security

All network communications are encrypted in transit using industry-standard Transport Layer Security (TLS/HTTPS). User data stored in Cloud Firestore is protected by role-based Firestore Security Rules that restrict data access solely to the authenticated owner matching the account UID.

5. Data Retention and Deletion Rights

We retain your study progress as long as your account remains active. You maintain complete control over your data:

  • Revoke Google Access: You can revoke LevelUp Study's access to your Google Account at any time via Google Account Security Permissions.
  • Account & Data Deletion: You can request full, permanent deletion of your account and all associated study data at any time by emailing us at madhavjaiswal236@gmail.com with the subject line "Data Deletion Request". Upon receipt, all records tied to your User ID will be permanently purged within 30 days.

6. Children's Privacy

LevelUp Study is designed for students preparing for competitive examinations (such as JEE and board exams). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 without parental consent. If we learn that an account has been created by a child under 13 without verification, we will promptly delete the data.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may periodically update this Privacy Policy to reflect application improvements or legal requirements. Any updates will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
