Transparent Google User Data Disclosure

LevelUp Study respects your privacy. We only request permissions necessary to provide study scheduling features. Below is a complete and transparent explanation of each Google OAuth permission we use:

Google Calendar (auth/calendar)

Purpose: Used solely to push scheduled study slots, revision blocks, and exam milestones into your Google Calendar with automated 15-minute rest breaks. We do not read, delete, or modify any unrelated events on your calendar.

Google Tasks (auth/tasks)

Purpose: Used to export your daily chapter goals, practice quotas, and backlog action items into Google Tasks so you can check them off from any device.

Google Account Profile & Email

Purpose: Used to securely sign you in and bind your personal study stats, streaks, and syllabus mastery to your account across web and mobile sessions.

Cloud Firestore Storage

Purpose: Stores your private syllabus completion records and XP logs protected by zero-trust security rules. Data is strictly isolated to your own User ID.

Google Limited Use Policy Compliance:
LevelUp Study's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres strictly to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We never sell user data, share data with third parties, or use your data for advertising or AI model training.
